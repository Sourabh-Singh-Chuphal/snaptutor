# 90-second demo script

**0:00 - Problem (15s)**
"Students get stuck on a problem at night with nobody to ask, and typing a diagram into a chatbot is painful."

**0:15 - Live demo (45s)**
1. Open SnapTutor on the phone. Take a photo of a handwritten or textbook problem.
2. Choose "Beginner", tap **Explain**. Scroll through the numbered steps and the "Common mistake" note.
3. Tap **Hint only** to show it nudges without spoiling.
4. Tap **Practice questions** and reveal one answer.

**1:00 - Why it is open (20s)**
"It runs on Gemma 4, an open-weights multimodal model. The tutoring behavior is a reusable Agent Skill in the repo, so anyone can drop it into their own agent. MIT licensed."

**1:20 - Close (10s)**
"Next: offline mode with Ollama and multi-language support. Repo link is on screen."
